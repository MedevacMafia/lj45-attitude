Privacy — LJ45 Attitude

This independent aviation-inspired web simulation uses device orientation, compass and location readings only on your device to draw the instrument panel. GPS altitude and speed require you to enable live GPS mode. The app does not transmit your coordinates to the developer, does not use analytics or ads, and does not maintain an account.

Files are served by GitHub Pages, which may process standard access logs according to GitHub's policies. The browser may cache app files for offline use. Location and sensor readings are not cached.

The app is not a flight instrument, not affiliated with any aircraft or avionics manufacturer, and must never be used for navigation.
